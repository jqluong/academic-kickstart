I completed my PhD in applied mathematics at UCLA in the Fall of 2026. I am interested in the mathematics arising from engineering and physical applications. I aim to use mathematical modelling and analysis, coupled with numerical simulations to shed further insight into physical phenomena to guide relevant application. Currently, I focus on compressible fluid dynamics, particularly gas dynamics modelled by the inviscid compressible Euler equations. I previously worked on particle laden flow and control theory for PDEs. My disseration, in conjunction with Los Alamos National Laboratory, focused on self-similar implosions for the isentropic, compressible Euler equations.

I completed my undergraduate degree at Fresno State in 2021, studying pure mathematics and mechanical engineering.
